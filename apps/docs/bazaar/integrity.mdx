---
title: "Catalog integrity"
description: "The facilitator is a trust boundary. Nobody can spoof another seller's listing or pricing, and a settled payTo owns its key."
---

Clients echo the resource block into the payment payload, so the facilitator treats every listing as untrusted input.

- **Ownership.** A listing belongs to the `payTo` that first settles a payment for it. A settled listing with a different owner cannot be overwritten (`bazaar_listing_ownership_conflict`); a provisional one is displaceable, so a free verify can never lock out a real seller.
- **Domain proof (SEP-1).** A `payTo` listed in the domain's `.well-known/stellar.toml` is marked `domainVerified`, can reclaim a squatted listing, and is never displaced.
- **Provable asset identity.** The asset on a listing is derived by the facilitator from the on-chain contract, not taken from the client, so a token cannot claim to be USDC when it is not.
- **Route templates** are percent-decoded before the path-traversal check, and service metadata is soft-dropped per field.
- **Anti-abuse.** Only distinct real payers move ranking; a self-payment earns nothing.
