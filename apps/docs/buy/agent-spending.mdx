---
title: "Agent spending"
description: "Cap what an autonomous agent spends: a hard spend cap on every paid call, and on-chain spending policies for smart-account agents."
---

An autonomous buyer needs a spending limit it cannot exceed. Rail402 enforces this in two places.

**A hard spend cap per call.** Every paid call takes a required `maxAmount`. The cap is enforced in the payment-requirements selector, immediately before signing, so a resource that quotes cheap and charges more is refused with nothing spent. See [Pay for an API](/buyer/quickstart) and the [MCP discovery server](/buyer/mcp-server).

**On-chain spending policies.** A smart-contract (`C...`) account can carry a spending policy that refuses an over-budget payment on chain, in the account's own `__check_auth`. With `upto`, the settlement reconciles the reserved ceiling down to the actual charge. See [Smart-account budgets](/schemes/smart-accounts).
