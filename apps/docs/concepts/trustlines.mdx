---
title: "Trustlines & SEP-41 assets"
description: "A Stellar account needs a trustline to a SEP-41 asset before it can hold it. Both the buyer and the seller need one."
---

Rail402 settles any SEP-41 token, with USDC as the default. Every SEP-41 asset is reachable from Soroban through its Stellar Asset Contract (SAC); the testnet USDC contract is `CBIELTK6YBZJU5UP2WWQEUCYKLPU6AUNZ2BQ4WWFEIE3USCIHMXQDAMA`.

A Stellar account needs a **trustline** to an asset before it can hold that asset, and both sides of a payment need one: the buyer to send, the seller to receive. The seller's is the one people forget, so it has its own rejection code:

| Missing trustline | Code |
| --- | --- |
| Seller cannot receive | `invalid_exact_stellar_payload_missing_trustline_recipient` |
| Buyer cannot send | `invalid_exact_stellar_payload_missing_trustline_payer` |

Amounts are integers at 7 decimals: one USDC is `10000000` atomic units, so `500000` is 0.05 USDC. Always pass amounts as integer strings.
